Your Rights When Purchasing Funeral Products

Under United States Federal Law enforced by the Federal Trade Commission (16 CFR Part 453):

  • Right to Purchase from Any Provider: Funeral homes and mortuaries cannot require you to purchase a casket or urn from them. You have the absolute federal legal right to buy from Eternum.
  • No Handling Fees or Surcharges: Funeral directors are strictly prohibited by federal law from charging any "handling fee," "receiving fee," or penalty for accepting a casket purchased from a third-party seller.
  • Delivery information: The FTC Funeral Rule addresses how funeral providers handle caskets purchased elsewhere. Contact your provider to confirm delivery details and requirements.
  • General Price List (GPL): Funeral homes must provide you with an itemized price list before showing you any caskets.

Rules and requirements can vary by situation. For advice about your rights, consult the FTC guidance or contact your state consumer protection office.
