Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about products, orders, delivery, and custom requests.

1. Delivery

How fast can Eternum deliver to my funeral home? expand_more

Products marked for immediate dispatch may be available to ship sooner. Delivery times depend on the item and destination. Contact us to discuss timing for your order.

Do I need to be present when the casket is delivered? expand_more

Usually, the funeral home can receive the delivery on your behalf. Please confirm its receiving arrangements and hours before placing your order.

2. FTC Funeral Rule

Can a funeral home refuse to accept a casket from Eternum? expand_more

The FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR Part 453) addresses funeral providers’ responsibilities when a customer purchases a casket elsewhere. Review the FTC’s current guidance or consult a consumer protection office for details about your situation.

Will using Eternum create friction with my funeral director? expand_more

You can share the product specifications and delivery details with your funeral director. Contact us if you need help finding that information.

3. Materials and Custom Requests

Are the eco-friendly willow and pine caskets approved for green cemeteries? expand_more

Check each product page for its materials and any listed certifications. Cemetery requirements can vary, so confirm that a product meets the requirements of your chosen cemetery.

Can I request custom lettering or a family crest? expand_more

Some products may support custom options. Use the contact form to ask about availability, design details, and timing.
