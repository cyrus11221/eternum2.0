Frequently Asked Questions

Guidance regarding delivery times, funeral home coordination, legal rights, and custom carving.

1. Delivery & Mortuary Logistics

How fast can Eternum deliver to my funeral home? expand_more

For all models marked with "Immediate Crypt Dispatch (< 24h)", we provide guaranteed overnight direct courier transit to any licensed mortuary or crematorium across North America. For urgent emergencies, please submit our 24/7 Urgent Enquiry form or call 1-800-ETERNUM immediately.

Do I need to be present when the casket is delivered? expand_more

No. Under FTC federal regulations, you are not required to be present. Our white-glove logistics team coordinates directly with the mortuary receiving manager, inspects the casket together with them, and signs the chain-of-custody transfer on your behalf.

2. FTC Funeral Rule & Legal Rights

Can a funeral home refuse to accept a casket from Eternum? expand_more

No. Federal Law (FTC Funeral Rule 16 CFR § 453) explicitly prohibits funeral homes from refusing to accept third-party caskets or charging any receiving or handling fee. Violating this law exposes the facility to severe federal fines.

Will using Eternum create friction with my funeral director? expand_more

Funeral directors are accustomed to third-party deliveries. Our counselors speak to funeral directors with collegial professional respect, providing full dimensions, weights, and scheduled delivery windows in advance.

3. Craftsmanship, Sizing & Green Burials

Are the eco-friendly willow and pine caskets approved for green cemeteries? expand_more

Yes. The Avalon Woven Willow and Serenity Forest Pine are certified by the Green Burial Council. They use 100% natural wood dowels or coppiced willow fibers with unbleached organic cotton, free of nails, metal screws, or synthetic varnishes.

Can I request bespoke monogramming or family crest carvings? expand_more

Yes. Our master woodcarving atelier offers personalized monogram reliefs, family crests, and scripture verse engravings. You can submit these through the "Request Custom Carving" link on any product page or via our Urgent Enquiry desk.
