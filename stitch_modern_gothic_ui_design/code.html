gavel Know Your Federal Legal Rights — The FTC Funeral Rule

You are never obligated to purchase a casket from a funeral home.

Under Federal Law (16 CFR Part 453), funeral providers must accept delivery of third-party caskets without charging any handling fee, surcharge, or penalty. Eternum delivers directly to any licensed mortuary or cemetery across North America with complete chain-of-custody documentation.
