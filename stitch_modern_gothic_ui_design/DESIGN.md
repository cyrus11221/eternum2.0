## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern gothic solemnity balanced with quiet luxury and uncompromised dignity. Built for individuals and families navigating legacy, bereavement, and memorial curation, the aesthetic moves away from clinical mortuary traditions and cheap sentimentality. Instead, it draws from high-end architectural reverence, artisanal craft, sacred geometry, and modern minimalism.

The visual style is characterized by:
- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep obsidian voids layered against textured charcoals, punctuated by the restrained glow of warm antique brass and candlelit alabaster.
- **Architectural Gothic Restraint:** Subtle structural cues—slender vertical proportions, lancet arch silhouettes in imagery framing, and hairline filigree borders—reinterpreted through sleek, contemporary precision.
- **Compassionate Reverence:** Calm, deliberate pacing with ample breathing room. The UI does not demand attention; it holds space, creating an environment that feels enduring, respectful, and timeless.

## Colors

The color system operates strictly in an elevated dark mode to sustain quiet focus and solemn luxury.

- **Primary (`#C5A880` - Burnished Brass):** Used for focal interactive moments, premium tier accents, refined borders, and key milestones. Avoid heavy, ungrounded fills; favor metallic sheen, hairline outlines, and delicate typographic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#8A7968` - Antique Bronze):** A muted metallic mid-tone for secondary actions, subdued icons, divider filigrees, and tertiary captions.
- **Tertiary (`#E6E1DA` - Warm Alabaster):** High-legibility text and soft highlights. This off-white carries a gentle limestone warmth, removing harsh digital glare.
- **Neutral (`#0D0D0E` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational canvas. Paired with tonal surfaces: Charcoal Slate (`#161618`), Crypt Stone (`#1F1F23`), and Sepulcher Grey (`#2A2A30`) to establish spatial depth without standard elevation drop shadows.

## Typography

The typography establishes a dialogue between historical reverence and razor-sharp modern legibility.

- **Display & Headings (EB Garamond):** Chosen for its humanist classical proportions, elegant serifs, and literary grace. Titles should embrace generous leading and occasional italicized dignity for quotes, epitaphs, and casket material descriptions.
- **Interface & Text (Hanken Grotesk):** A crisp, structured, modern grotesque that provides immediate clarity for technical specifications, dimensions, logistics, and pricing.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** All small labels and section indicators use `Hanken Grotesk` uppercase tracking (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) to evoke chiseled stone inscriptions without compromising functional legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured on a 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Pacing & Breathing Room:** Whitespace—or rather, deep obsidian space—is treated as an active emotional buffer. Margins are generous, giving users room to breathe and process decisions without visual aggression.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Section divisions lean on extended vertical spacing (`space-2xl`) and delicate hairline gold/bronze separators rather than blocky container backgrounds.
- **Reflow Rules:** Complex product configurations (casket wood types, lining fabrics, bespoke engravings) shift from side-by-side split screens on desktop to clean, sequential, vertically stacked cards on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the sacred, grounded quality of the gothic atmosphere, the design avoids artificial drop shadows and skeuomorphic blur stacks. Visual hierarchy is established via **architectural tonal layering** and **low-contrast metallic borders**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Base background in `#0D0D0E`.
- **Layer 1 (Card/Section):** Deep Charcoal `#161618` bordered by a 1px hairline stroke of `#8A7968` at 20% opacity.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays/Modals):** Crypt Stone `#1F1F23` with a delicate 1px border of `#C5A880` at 40% opacity, supplemented by an ambient warm black shadow: `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
- **Backdrop Treatments:** Modals and drawer backdrops utilize a dark veil of `rgba(13, 13, 14, 0.85)` with a subtle 6px blur to maintain focus on the ritual interaction.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a crisp, razor-sharp architectural geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Orthogonal Precision:** Cards, input fields, interactive badges, and buttons utilize strict 90-degree corners, echoing traditional stonework, sarcophagi, and classical mausoleum construction.
- **Gothic Arch Archetypes:** While interactive functional components remain square, curated image portals, media masks, and featured memorial headers may utilize single-ended gothic arch profiles (rounded top lancet curves paired with square flat bottoms) to evoke cathedral vaulting and solemn sanctuary windows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Sharp-cornered solid `#C5A880` (Burnished Brass) with `#0D0D0E` bold label text in uppercase grotesque. Hover transitions shift subtly to `#D8C2A0` with a fine external outline offset by 2px.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed in a 1px border of `#8A7968`. Text rendered in `#E6E1DA`. Hover gently illuminates the background with `#C5A880` at 10% opacity.
- **Minimal Text Actions:** Underlined with a delicate hairline brass stroke offset by 4px, transitioning to full opacity on hover.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Form Fields:** Understated baseline-only or full thin-bordered rectangular boxes in `#161618` with a 1px border of `#2A2A30`. On focus, the border shifts smoothly to `#C5A880` without glowing drop shadows.
- **Dropdowns & Pickers:** Modal-grade panels floating with crisp edges, showcasing materials (e.g., Solid Walnut, Cast Bronze, Heritage Oak) with rich tactile swatches framed in thin brass.

### Cards & Memorial Showcases
- **Casket & Urn Displays:** Set against `#161618` with 1px border outlines. Imagery is granted primary emphasis, surrounded by restrained typographic specifications and alabaster pricing details.
- **Interactive Memorial Tiles:** Incorporate subtle filigree corner anchors (1px gold cross-hair or architectural notched borders) that brighten upon hover.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Square outer boundary with an inner square brass diamond glyph when active, departing from modern rounded circles toward geometric relic-like precision.
- **Checkboxes:** Monolithic square box, filled with a precise hairline brass checkmark upon selection.

### Memorial Tribute & Memory Reel (Product-Specific)
- **Epitaph Inscriptions:** Text preview panels rendered with serif typography, accompanied by fine gold corner accents.
- **Timeline Milestones:** A continuous hairline vertical brass rule connecting dates and events, anchored by diamond-shaped indicators.
